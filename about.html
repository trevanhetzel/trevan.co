About me

Hey, I'm Trevan (pronounced as if the A were an I). I design and build websites and help local businesses get more leads online. These days I run Hetzel Creative full time, the small consulting business I started back in 2008, where I work with business owners to grow through web design, local SEO, and paid ads.

My focus is simple: get you found and turn more of those visitors into customers. That means building fast, great-looking websites, dialing in your local SEO so you show up when nearby customers search, and running paid ad campaigns that actually pay for themselves. I love working with scrappy teams and owners who care about their craft, and I have a penchant for making sure everything looks and feels really good. I'm always learning new tech, but I have the most fun with JavaScript, React, Astro, CSS, Ruby (especially on Rails), PHP, and WordPress, depending on the occasion.

A bit of background

Before going all in on Hetzel Creative, I spent years building software for fast growing startups and large brands. In 2021, I played a key role in rebuilding Parler after it was deplatformed, and later worked on Truth Social, building software to promote open and free global conversation. Earlier on, I got to write a lot of the code behind brands like Rent Like A Champion, which landed on Shark Tank after their investment from Mark Cuban and Chris Sacca, along with Bloomberg, Zumba, and a Celebrity Cruises responsive redesign. That experience is what I now bring to local businesses.
